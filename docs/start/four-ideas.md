# Four ideas

Four words are the whole tool.

A **boundary** is where two units of code meet: an endpoint and its
caller, a query and a table, a resolver and a schema.

A **summary** is what suss worked out about one unit: which branches it
takes, under what conditions, what each one produces, and what it
touched on the way.

A **check** compares the summaries on either side of a boundary.

A **pack** teaches suss a library, and one ships for most of the stack
already.
